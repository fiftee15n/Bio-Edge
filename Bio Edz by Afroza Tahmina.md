# **Bio Edz by Afroza Tahmina**

## **Premium HEC Biology Intensive Program**

### **Complete Website, Student Portal & Teacher Management System Specification**

---

# **1\. PROJECT OVERVIEW**

Build a modern, clean, minimalist and highly user-friendly educational web platform for:

**Bio Edz by Afroza Tahmina**

The flagship course/program will be:

**Premium HEC Biology Intensive Program**

The program is a **4-month intensive Biology preparation program** covering both:

* HEC Biology First Paper  
* HEC Biology Second Paper

The program is designed to provide students with a structured, focused and result-oriented preparation system.

The platform should not feel like a generic coaching website.

It should feel like a **premium digital learning environment specifically designed for Biology students**.

The entire experience should communicate:

* Academic quality  
* Structure  
* Discipline  
* Personal attention  
* Progress  
* Confidence  
* Biology/natural-science identity

---

# **2\. BRAND IDENTITY**

## **Brand**

**Bio Edz**

## **Brand descriptor**

**by Afroza Tahmina**

## **Program**

**Premium HEC Biology Intensive Program**

## **Program positioning**

A structured and focused Biology preparation program covering both First Paper and Second Paper through intensive classes, regular assessments, model tests, practice and individual feedback.

## **Visual direction**

Use a very clean, modern and soft visual identity.

Primary visual direction:

* White  
* Very light green  
* Soft natural green  
* Dark green for important text  
* Very subtle gray  
* Optional muted green accent

The design should create a:

**Natural \+ Academic \+ Premium \+ Calm**

visual atmosphere.

Avoid making it look like:

* A hospital website  
* A generic school website  
* A gaming platform  
* A colorful children's education platform  
* A corporate SaaS dashboard

---

# **3\. DESIGN SYSTEM**

## **Overall design language**

Use:

* Minimalism  
* Generous whitespace  
* Soft rounded cards  
* Subtle borders  
* Very light shadows  
* Clear typography hierarchy  
* Consistent spacing  
* Soft green highlights  
* Simple icons  
* Clean charts  
* Responsive layouts

Avoid excessive:

* Gradients  
* Animations  
* Glassmorphism  
* Heavy shadows  
* Large decorative illustrations  
* Too many colors  
* Excessive icons  
* Visual clutter

The interface should feel premium even though it is simple.
